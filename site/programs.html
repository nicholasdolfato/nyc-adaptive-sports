Adaptive Sports Programs in NYC

Below is information about four top youth adaptive sports programs in New York City. Click to learn more about each program and what they offer for your child!

KEEN (Kids Enjoy Exercise Now)

  • Ages 5–21 with physical and developmental disabilities
  • Free, volunteer-run, non-competitive
  • Tailored to each athlete's needs
  • Seasonal: fall, winter, spring, summer

Mission: Empower youth with disabilities and impact volunteer coaches through participation in free, non-competitive, one-on-one programs of physical activity and fun, supporting the overall health and well-being of all participants.

Fall Season Programs

  • KEEN Sports: loosely-structured sports activities and free play opportunities — hoops, scooters, t-ball, soccer, relay, ball play, and more
  • KEEN Yoga: poses taught in a way accessible to all athletes, with modifications for individual needs; music and stories incorporated
  • KEEN Swim: swim, splash, and play in the water with life jackets and pool toys; supervised, no prior swim training needed — rarely instructional, mostly for getting used to the water
  • KEEN Fit: a fitness program for teens, with each athlete paired with a volunteer to assist with exercises
  • KEEN Basketball: shooting, ball handling, and footwork in a team setting, plus 1-on-1 sessions between athlete and volunteer
  • KEEN Dance: a session leader facilitates dance options and guides athletes through movement exercises
  • Also offers cooking classes and online classes, plus other sports like flag football and soccer

Online sessions are Fridays at 5pm for Dance; in-person activities run all day Saturdays and Sundays.

Contact: P.O. Box 5115, New York, NY 10185 917-830-5725 · info@keennewyork.org

For more info, visit keennewyork.org

Play4Autism

  • Overall goal: Connecting Children with Autism to sports and recreational activities
  • Contact at 347-736-0025
  • Description from Website: Play4Autism offers a variety of programs designed to help children & adults on the autism spectrum develop social, communication, and leadership skills through sports and recreational activities. These programs are part of the "Kidz into Action" initiative, which aims to improve the self-esteem and social interaction of children & adults on the spectrum through interactive activities in a healthy, loving, and inclusive environment.
  • Kidz Into Action Program:

    • Ages 3+
    • Programs designed to create inclusive community for children and adults. Individuals w/ autism and people with diverse abilities can thrive in a supportive environment.
