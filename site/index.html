Sports for Every Child in NYC

Empowering Sports for Every Child, No Matter Their Athletic Ability

This site is to help find a place for kids who are in the "in-between". It is to find sports for kids who can join in the play, but need a little extra time, support, or flexibility, and who often feel out of place in typical athletic programs, but don't require high-support needs.

Why Adaptive Sports Matter

Adaptive sports are more than just physical activity; they also serve as a gateway to social connection and self-confidence. Through my research into NYC's youth programs, I've seen how tailored opportunities transform developmental trajectories. Beyond play, our parent chatboards act as a community pulse, empowering families to organize tournaments and events that ensure every child, regardless of capability, can flourish in a supportive team environment.
